how it works

for users

  1. Run omarket — a TUI, browse the whole catalog.
  2. Try anything, free. Buy what's worth paying for with omarket buy <app>.
  3. Your license key lands on disk. Nothing to log into, ever.

for devs

  1. Onboard with Stripe: omarket dev onboard -email you@x.com.
  2. Package your app — a PKGBUILD, optionally a license check.
  3. Open a PR adding catalog/<id>.json. That's the review process.
